The green form and licence application

How to begin applying for a driving licence in Israel.

What is the green form?

“Green form” is the familiar name for the new driving licence application. The process begins online with personal details and a health declaration. A photo and eye examination may also be required through an approved provider.

  1. Submit the online application and choose the licence category.
  2. Complete the photograph and eye test as instructed.
  3. Check your application status, then prepare for theory and practical lessons.
