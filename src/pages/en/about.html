About Alfred Kour

Driving instructor and theory lecturer since 2006.

Teaching approach

Alfred explains why road rules matter and connects theory questions to real traffic situations. Lessons focus on hazard awareness, sound judgment and safe driving habits, alongside preparation for the tests.

He is a qualified driving instructor and theory lecturer and holds a driving examiner certificate.

Experience and approach

Alfred has a bachelor’s degree in Business Administration from 2002. His lessons also cover vehicle checks, parking and reversing, coping with changing road conditions, and driving at a pace that builds confidence.
