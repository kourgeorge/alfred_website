New drivers and accompaniment

Check the conditions that apply after passing the practical test.

After passing

A young new driver may have an accompaniment period depending on age and the permit conditions. Check your permit for the applicable duration, hours, who may accompany you and any restrictions.

At the end of the period, a completion declaration may be required. Always confirm the current official conditions before driving or submitting the declaration.
