Managing theory-test stress

Practical ways to study without burning out.

Make study manageable

Continuous study under pressure can make concentration harder. Split the material into sessions, take planned breaks and use questions to identify what needs more review.

  1. Get enough sleep before the test.
  2. Eat regular meals and limit caffeine.
  3. Divide study into manageable blocks with breaks.
  4. Plan a short enjoyable activity at the end of the day.
  5. Try moderate movement, such as a walk.
  6. Replace discouraging self-talk with reminders of what you have learned.
  7. Remember that the exam period is temporary; protect your wellbeing.
