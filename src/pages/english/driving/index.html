Driving lessons

Practical instruction that develops safe habits beyond the driving test.

Learn to read the road

Lessons combine vehicle control with observation, appropriate speed, lane position, junctions and sound decisions. Alfred adapts the pace to each learner and prepares them for both the practical test and everyday driving.

Ask about manual or automatic lessons, refresher lessons, foreign licence conversion and returning to driving after a break.

Progress beyond the test

A student record tracks vehicle operation, road understanding and traffic skills. Guided practice includes reading other road users’ intentions, anticipating hazards and driving in varied conditions such as hills, bends and at night.
