Before the practical driving test

Arrive prepared and focus on the driving habits you have learned.

The day before

Discuss any uncertain situations with your instructor, then allow time to rest. Sleep, regular meals and a plan for reaching the test location can make the day less stressful.

  • Recall observation, suitable speed and safe decisions.
  • Ask your instructor for focused feedback.
  • Prepare identification and anything else required in your official appointment.
  • Avoid exhausting last-minute practice.

Keep perspective

The test is one stage in learning to drive. Take breaks, limit caffeine and concentrate on the next safe decision. Learn about the driving lessons →
