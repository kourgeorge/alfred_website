Online theory course

Learn Israeli road rules, signs and safe driving with video lessons and practice.

What you will learn

The course covers traffic law and signs, priority, correct driving, hazard awareness and road safety. It links theory questions to decisions made behind the wheel.

  • Traffic laws, signs and right of way.
  • Speed, turning, observation and anticipating danger.
  • Vehicle systems, safety and test preparation.
